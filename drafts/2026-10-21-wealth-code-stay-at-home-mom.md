## Caption
The stay-at-home mom is not free.

So many families build their whole protection plan around the paycheck. Life insurance on the parent who earns it, disability coverage through work, maybe a policy from years ago. And the parent at home? Often nothing, because "she doesn't have an income to replace."

But think about what actually stops if she can't do it anymore. The childcare. The driving. The meals. The appointments. The calendar that runs the whole house. Someone would have to be paid to do all of it, often for years.

That's not a reason to feel guilty or scared. It's a reason to see your work clearly. Write down what you do in a week, price what it would cost to replace, and then have the coverage conversation together.

Your work doesn't come with a paycheck. It still holds everything together.

Send this to a mom who needs to hear it.

This is for educational purposes only and is not individualized financial, tax, or legal advice. Life insurance guarantees are based on the claims-paying ability of the issuing insurance company. Coverage is subject to underwriting, and riders may carry additional cost.
