# Do You Know Where It All Is?

## Story map
Hero: a woman who wants to feel in control of her future | Problem: ext: she doesn't know every account, beneficiary, or password; int: "He handles that"; phil: no one should learn what they have at the worst possible moment | Guide: compassion ("not a character flaw") + a simple method | Plan: one list with every account, where it's held, beneficiary, who to call | CTA: reflective question | Stakes (opportunity): knowing is protection | Success: she knows where every dollar is

## Hook options
1. **If something happened tomorrow, would you know where it all is?** (chosen)
2. The 401(k). The old IRA. The passwords. Do you know where they are?
3. "He handles that." Okay. But do you know where it is?

## Carousel copy (8 slides)
SLIDE 1: If something happened tomorrow, would you know where it all is?
SLIDE 2: The 401(k). The life insurance. The old IRA. The passwords. The safe deposit key.
SLIDE 3: So many women find out what they have at the worst possible moment.
SLIDE 4: Not knowing isn't a character flaw. It's usually a habit: "He handles that."
SLIDE 5: Knowing is a form of PROTECTION.
SLIDE 6: Start a simple list: every account, where it's held, who the beneficiary is, and who to call.
SLIDE 7: Then ask: Is my name on it? Is the beneficiary current? Would someone I trust know where to find this?
SLIDE 8: You don't have to manage every dollar. You do deserve to know where every dollar is. WEALTH CODE: WOMEN'S EDITION

## Caption
If something happened tomorrow, would you know where it all is?

A lot of women don't, and it's not because they aren't capable. It's because somewhere along the way, the money became someone else's job. Then life changes, and the learning happens all at once.

This week, start one list. Every account, where it's held, who the beneficiary is, and who to call. That's it. Knowing is a form of protection.

Who in your life would know where to find your list?

This is for educational purposes only and is not individualized financial, tax, or legal advice.
