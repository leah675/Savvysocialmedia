## 2. Accuracy fixes before these go to compliance

| Post | Issue | Suggested fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Fri Oct 23, The Medicare timeline**: "Miss this window and the penalty can last a lifetime." | Posted during Annual Open Enrollment (Oct 15 to Dec 7), this hook implies missing *that* window triggers a lifetime penalty. Lifetime late-enrollment penalties generally come from missing your Initial Enrollment Period (or a Special Enrollment Period) for Part B or Part D, not from skipping the annual window. Compliance is likely to flag it as misleading. | "Medicare has more than one enrollment window. Mixing them up can be expensive." Then teach the difference: Initial Enrollment (around 65), Annual Open Enrollment (Oct 15 to Dec 7, changes take effect Jan 1), and when late penalties apply. Cite Medicare.gov. |
| **Fri Oct 30, Health insurance before 65** | The enhanced ACA premium tax credits were scheduled to expire after 2025, which brings back the subsidy "cliff" at 400% of the federal poverty level unless Congress changed it. Confirm current law right before submitting. | Keep the hook. In "Facts to verify," cite HealthCare.gov for the 2027 plan-year rules. |
| **Fri Dec 18, Charitable giving when you don't itemize** | The new non-itemizer deduction (from the 2025 tax law) starts with tax year 2026. Confirm the amounts (expected $1,000 single / $2,000 married filing jointly), that it applies to cash gifts to qualifying public charities (donor-advised funds generally don't qualify), and the new 0.5% of AGI floor for itemizers. | Keep "New for 2026," and add the source (IRS) in the caption or newsletter. |
| **Fri Jan 8, The 0% capital gains bracket** | Thresholds change every year, and in January people may mix up the tax year they're filing with the current year. | Say which tax year the thresholds apply to and cite the IRS. Label every example hypothetical. |
| **Fri Apr 9, Before April 15: what you can still contribute for 2026** | Limits change yearly (2026 IRA, catch-up, and HSA limits). HSA eligibility rules matter (must have had HDHP coverage). | Cite IRS limits. Note that eligibility rules apply. |
| **Fri Apr 30, Special needs planning** | SSI and ABLE rules (including the ABLE age expansion to disability onset before 46, effective 2026) should be cited. | Cite SSA.gov and ABLE National Resource Center; attorney referral line. |
| **Wed Feb 17 and Mar 3** | "Catch-up rules were written for you": catch-up contributions start at 50, with a higher catch-up for ages 60 to 63 (SECURE 2.0) and, starting 2026, Roth-only catch-ups for higher earners. | Fine as a hook. Make sure the body is accurate on the age bands and Roth rule, and cite the IRS. |
